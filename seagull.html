Take Back Your Island

The humans are turning your island into their vacation spot — it's time to cause a Gulltastrophe!

Fly, Dash and Poop your way to victory in this funny 3D seagull action game. Explore the island, steal food and wage gullrilla warfare against the invaders.

Combine unique sequences of food to unleash hilarious Poop combos, or show who's top of the pecking order by turning your environment to your advantage. But watch out for the police — they're out in numbers and only too eager to send you squawking!

Crappy Gameplay

At the core of Gulltastrophe is its highly sophisticated pooping mechanic. Discover different sequences of foods and their ramifications when dropped from a great height. Discharge scorching and spicy, thunderously explosive or wonderfully whiffy deliveries down below and watch the chaos unfurl. Bombs away!

Physics Sandbox Adventure

Use your inherent knowledge of the island to turn the tide against these beachcombers. Kick over sandcastles, blow away picnics and send coconuts tumbling onto unsuspecting holidaymakers. Anyone would think you're enjoying this!

Two Gulls, One Coup

What's more intimidating than a seagull at the beach? A flock of seagulls. Swoop in with your pals and play solo or co-op across all game modes!
